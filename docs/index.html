Product design process

How the work moves from ambiguity to implementation.

01 ResearchQuestions, hypotheses, competitive context, and validation plans.
02 User FlowsJourneys, information architecture, and primary/secondary tasks.
03 WireframesLow-fidelity exploration before visual polish.
04 Design SystemTokens, components, states, responsive rules, and accessibility.
05 PrototypeInteractive flows that test behavior, not only appearance.
06 Usability TestingTask plans and evidence gathering; real findings are never fabricated.
07 IterationDesign changes tied to usability, hierarchy, accessibility, or product needs.
08 HandoffStates, semantics, responsive specs, and developer-ready notes.

How I work

Understand → Structure → Explore → Design → Validate → Handoff

The goal is not to decorate a solution. It is to clarify the problem, make the interaction understandable, test assumptions honestly, and give development enough detail to preserve the design intent.
